Thermostat Operation Modes
<w:left w:val="nil"/><w:bottom w:val="nil"/><w:right w:val="nil"/><w:between w:val="nil"/></w:pBdr></w:pPr><w:r><w:t>The operation of a temperature controller is basically divided into three modes: manual setting/scheduling and PWM. The selection screen is as follows.

2.6.1 Manual Operation Mode (Manual Mode)
<w:left w:val="nil"/><w:bottom w:val="nil"/><w:right w:val="nil"/><w:between w:val="nil"/></w:pBdr><w:spacing w:after="240"/></w:pPr><w:r><w:t>In Manual Mode, the thermostat regulates heating output primarily based on the temperature specified during Setting Mode. However, operations remain constrained by environmental boundaries such as high/low protection limits, window open detection, and continuous heating limitations (Power Limit).

2.6.2 Schedule Mode
<w:left w:val="nil"/><w:bottom w:val="nil"/><w:right w:val="nil"/><w:between w:val="nil"/></w:pBdr></w:pPr><w:r><w:t>The thermostat offers editable scheduling options, allowing users to predefine execution times and target temperatures. Once Schedule Mode is activated, the system executes heating according to these configurations. Schedule parameters can be modified under the Heating Schedule menu. When active, the main display UI changes: the standard "M" icon disappears and is replaced by a "PX" icon.


2.6.3 PWM Mode
<w:left w:val="nil"/><w:bottom w:val="nil"/><w:right w:val="nil"/><w:between w:val="nil"/></w:pBdr></w:pPr><w:r><w:t>When operating in PWM Mode, the relay no longer references target temperature values for switching logic. Instead, it relies on a predefined PWM Duty cycle to dictate activation durations, framing operations strictly around time intervals. The system executes switching intervals based on a 20-minute cycle block. For example, a 50% PWM Duty setting instructs the relay to engage for 10 minutes and disengage for 10 minutes. Operation in PWM mode remains subject to external conditions, including high/low protection limits and window open detection. When enabled, the main interface updates to display the PWM mode wave icon.

Control Parameter Settings (Control Adjustment)
<w:left w:val="nil"/><w:bottom w:val="nil"/><w:right w:val="nil"/><w:between w:val="nil"/></w:pBdr><w:spacing w:after="240"/></w:pPr><w:r><w:t>The Control Adjustment menu contains eight parameters to optimize system performance:
2.8.1 Main Sensor Selection
<w:left w:val="nil"/><w:bottom w:val="nil"/><w:right w:val="nil"/><w:between w:val="nil"/></w:pBdr></w:pPr><w:r w:rsidRPr="006D6A55"><w:t>This option allows you to choose which sensor is used to control the heating system. Conclusion and Maintenance Recommendations
This Nordic Edition Electric Underfloor Heating Thermostat integrates an intuitive dynamic three-state color feedback mechanism (switching between black and blue fonts) and a unified Top Bar interface framework. These features are designed to significantly streamline daily configuration paths while effectively mitigating the risk of accidental adjustments. 
Furthermore, the system’s embedded Output Power Limit and dual protection temperature mechanisms (Overheating & Frost Protection Limits) provide essential micro-environmental physical protection tailored for the wooden flooring structures widely used in the Nordic market. This reliably prevents progressive thermal damage to floorboards caused by localized heat accumulation beneath rugs or low-profile furniture. For daily operation, it is recommended to flexibly configure the scheduling profiles (5+2, 6+1, or 7-day modes), frost protection thresholds (Input Temp. Limit), and energy-saving backlight parameters according to your specific interior layouts and seasonal demands. This ensures the underfloor heating system continuously operates in its safest, most efficient, and energy-optimized state.

⚠️ SAFETY WARNINGS & INSTALLATION GUIDELINES
Authorized Personnel Only :
In accordance with Norwegian regulations (FEK), this thermostat must only be installed, connected, or serviced by a registered electrical installation enterprise (authorized electrician). DIY installation is strictly prohibited and will void all warranties.
Compliance with Standards :
The entire installation must comply with the Norwegian electrical standard NEK 400 and local codes.
Power Isolation & Dual-Pole Disconnection :
Always disconnect the main power supply before working on the system. For installations in wet rooms (bathrooms, laundries, etc.), a dual-pole disconnection (allpolig brudd) is required. Ensure the heating system is completely de-energized.
RCD Protection :
The heating circuit connected to this thermostat must be protected by a residual current device (RCD) with a rated operating current not exceeding 30 mA.
Floor Sensor Protection for Timber Floors :
When installing heating cables under timber, parquet, or laminate floors, the external NTC floor sensor must be connected and configured with a maximum temperature limit (typically 27°C) to prevent floor damage, in compliance with EN 50559.
